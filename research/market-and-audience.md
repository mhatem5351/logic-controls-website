# Logic Controls — Market & Audience Research

_Prepared 2026-10-06 for the logic-controls.com rebuild. Every figure carries an inline source link; the full list is at the end. Where a figure comes from a vendor blog, a developer's marketing page or a search-engine summary I could not open in full, it is flagged **(weak source)** or **(unverified)**._

> **Constraint from the lead (applied throughout):** launch languages are **decided — Arabic (default, RTL), English, Simplified Chinese (zh-CN), Turkish (tr)**. Section 2 is written as an evidence check on that choice, plus what would justify a later 5th language. The site must stay **$0 forever**, so every channel or tool recommended here is free (no paid WeChat Service Account, no paid WhatsApp API, no paid translation platforms).

## TL;DR

1. **Factories are growing fast.** Non-petroleum manufacturing grew **9 % in FY2025/26** and was the biggest single contributor to GDP growth (5.1 %) ([Ahram, 2026-09](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/575902.aspx)). Net FDI was **$9.3 bn in H1 FY2025/26**, up from about $6 bn a year earlier ([FinancialAfrik, 2026-04](https://www.financialafrik.com/en/2026/04/24/egypt-fdi-reach-9-3-billion-in-the-first-half-of-2025-2026/)).
2. **China is the biggest source of new factories.** It was Egypt's largest source of greenfield FDI in H1 2026, with **$4.9 bn** of a $12.4 bn total ([Enterprise citing Emirates NBD Research, 2026-09-10](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/09/10/egypt-and-india-propped-up-the-regions-rising-share-of-global-fdi-in-1h-2026/)). Chinese firms account for **about 50 % of the $11.6 bn** that SCZONE attracted over 3.5 years ([Amwal Al Ghad, 2025-11](https://en.amwalalghad.com/?p=222829)).
3. **Turkish investors are concentrated where Logic Controls already works.** There are about **1,700 Turkish companies, more than $3 bn invested and roughly 200 factories**, clustered in **10th of Ramadan, Sadat City and SCZONE** ([Enterprise, 2026-04-23](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/04/23/egypts-investment-labor-ministries-roll-out-the-red-carpet-for-major-foreign-companies/)).
4. **The data supports the ar/en/zh-CN/tr set.** China and Turkey are the two largest non-Arab sources of *manufacturing* FDI. Both countries sit in EF's **"Low" English-proficiency band**. Gulf investors are covered by the Arabic pages, and Indian and German investors by the English pages.
5. **What foreign investors need is a local partner that removes risk.** Their worries are skilled-technician shortages, FX and cost swings, imported-parts lead times, commissioning imported lines, standards (IEC/CE, plus the new **EU Machinery Regulation from 20 Jan 2027**), after-sales support, and documentation in their own language.
6. **What local buyers need is speed and trust signals.** They expect Arabic first, a mobile-friendly site (mobile is **about 64 % of Egyptian web traffic**), WhatsApp/phone contact, a fast reply, real photos and references, visible local presence by governorate, and price/quality options.

### 1.2 SCZONE (Suez Canal Economic Zone)

- **Footprint:** about 455 km², with 4 industrial zones (Sokhna, East Port Said, Qantara West, East Ismailia) and 6 ports ([DLA Piper, 2026-02](https://www.dlapiper.com/en/insights/publications/2026/02/investment-considerations-in-suez-canal-economic-zone)).
- **FY2025/26:** **117 new contracted projects worth $7.26 bn**, expected to create about **73,500 direct jobs** on 8.7 m m². The cumulative total is about **$16 bn** over four years, with 398 projects in the industrial zones plus 14 in the ports ([Ahram, 2026-07-16](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/572953.aspx)). Amwal Al Ghad puts the total at 412 projects and $16.4 bn ([Amwal Al Ghad](https://en.amwalalghad.com/?p=224360)).
- **Factories on the ground:** **212 operating and 176 under construction (about 400 in total)**. About 140 of the 212 opened in the last four years (PM Madbouly; [Ahram, 2026-09-06](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/576093.aspx)).
- **Chinese weight:**
  - Chinese firms account for about **50 % of $11.6 bn** of SCZONE FDI, and SCZONE aims to host **1,000 Chinese companies by 2030** ([Amwal Al Ghad](https://en.amwalalghad.com/?p=222829); [Enterprise](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2025/12/24/chinese-manufacturers-are-investing-another-usd-1-2-bn-in-manufacturing-here/)).
  - **TEDA (China-Egypt) zone** in Sokhna: more than **200 companies**, more than **$4.7 bn** invested and more than **10,000 jobs** by June 2026. A Phase 3 expansion takes it to about 10 km², targeting renewable-energy equipment, auto parts, textiles, polyester, tyres and healthcare products ([Ahram, 2026-09](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/575828.aspx)).
- **Typical new projects (automation-heavy):**
  - **Tyres:** Linglong $2 bn complex ([Ahram](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/576093.aspx)); Chaoyang Langma $190 m; Zenith $300 m tyre components/steel wire ([Ahram](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/572071.aspx)).
  - **Polyester:** Xin Feng Ming $800 m (1 Mt/yr).
  - **Hygiene products:** Tongling Jieya $160 m (wet wipes, diapers, nonwovens) ([Enterprise, 2025-12-24](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2025/12/24/chinese-manufacturers-are-investing-another-usd-1-2-bn-in-manufacturing-here/)).
- **Qantara West = textile hub:** **42 projects, $1.14 bn** ([Enterprise, 2026-04-23](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/04/23/egypts-investment-labor-ministries-roll-out-the-red-carpet-for-major-foreign-companies/)).
- **Incentives:**
  - 0 % customs and VAT on inputs.
  - 50 % income-tax deduction on net profits, capped at 80 % of paid-in capital over a maximum of 7 years.
  - One-stop shop, reduced utility costs and training subsidies ([SCZONE — Why SCZONE](https://sczone.eg/works/why-sczone/)).
  - 100 % foreign ownership, 5-year renewable residency, foreigners allowed up to 10 % of the workforce, land leases up to 50 years ([DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en/insights/publications/2026/02/investment-considerations-in-suez-canal-economic-zone)).
- **Russian Industrial Zone (East Port Said):** a developer was selected in July 2026. Build-out runs 2026–2029, with **operations around 2030** ([Ahram](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/572902.aspx)). This is not a near-term audience.

### 1.3 GAFI, the Golden License and incentives

- **Golden License:** a single Cabinet-issued approval that covers setting up, operating and managing a project, including building licences and land allocation (Investment Law 72/2017) ([Andersen Egypt](https://eg.andersen.com/the-golden-license-for-investment-projects/); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com:443/economic-analysis/1249674/the-golden-license-for-investment-projects)). Cabinet Decree 56/2022 criteria include ≥50 % exports within 3 years, foreign-currency financing, or ≥50 % local content to reduce imports ([Mondaq summary](https://webiis10.mondaq.com/contracts-and-commercial-law/1791596/golden-license-egypt-%7c-how-to-get-single-approval-for-projects)).
- **Momentum:**
  - **8 Golden Licenses on 2026-03-15** (≈$1.2 bn + EGP 16 bn). They include a Chinese stationery plant and Medlog's dry port in **10th of Ramadan**, a Chinese linen plant and an agro-processing plant in **Sadat City**, and MAC's vehicle plant in **New 6th of October** ([Arab Finance (AR)](https://arabfinance.com/ar/news/newdetails/منح-8-رخص-ذهبية-لمشروعات-باستثمارات-مليار-ومئتا-ألف-دولار)).
  - A 2025 batch of 9 included **Otsuka's $40 m food plant in 10th of Ramadan** ([Business Today Egypt](https://businesstodayegypt.com/Tag/65660/Golden-License/1)).
  - The government targets **80 more Golden Licenses in 4 years** ([Al Arabiya, 2026-02-10](https://www.alarabiya.net/aswaq/exclusive/2026/02/10/مصر-تستهدف-منح-80-رخصة-ذهبية-لاقامة-مشروعات-ضخمة-خلال-4-سنوات)).
- **Investment-cost deductions (Decree 503/2026, 2026-02-25):**
  - **Sector A** (underserved areas, Upper Egypt, New Capital, economic zones): **50 %** of investment cost deductible from taxable profits.
  - **Sector B:** **30 %**.
  - Both are capped at 80 % of paid-in capital over 7 years.
  - Newly prioritised products include vehicles, electric motors, refrigeration components, appliance metal sheet, pipes and fruit/vegetable concentrates ([Amwal Al Ghad](https://en.amwalalghad.com/?p=225542)).
  - A **cash incentive of 35–55 %** also exists for specific industrial projects ([Andersen Egypt](https://eg.andersen.com/investment-law/)).
- **New public free zones** planned by end-2026: **10th of Ramadan, New October, New Borg El Arab, New Alamein** ([AGBI, 2025-08](https://www.agbi.com/trade/2025/08/egypt-targets-140bn-exports-with-four-new-free-zones/)).
- **Red-tape relief:** in April 2026 the Investment and Labour ministries opened a direct-access unit for major foreign companies. LG had complained about residency and labour-dispute delays ([Enterprise, 2026-04-23](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/04/23/egypts-investment-labor-ministries-roll-out-the-red-carpet-for-major-foreign-companies/)).

### 2.2 Data that supports zh-CN

- China is the **largest source of greenfield FDI into Egypt in H1 2026 ($4.9 bn)** ([Enterprise](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/09/10/egypt-and-india-propped-up-the-regions-rising-share-of-global-fdi-in-1h-2026/)) and supplies **about half of SCZONE's foreign investment** ([Amwal Al Ghad](https://en.amwalalghad.com/?p=222829)).
- **TEDA alone hosts 200+ companies** and is expanding to about 10 km² ([Ahram](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/575828.aspx)). SCZONE targets **1,000 Chinese companies by 2030** ([Enterprise](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2025/12/24/chinese-manufacturers-are-investing-another-usd-1-2-bn-in-manufacturing-here/)).
- **US tariffs are pushing relocation.** Egyptian factory wages run about $100–150/month and Egypt faces a 10 % US tariff, against 30 %+ for Chinese goods ([Egyptian Streets, 2025-07](https://egyptianstreets.com/2025/07/21/chinese-investment-accelerates-in-egypt-amid-u-s-tariff-pressures/)).

  - China sits in EF's **Low** English band ([WPR/EF](https://worldpopulationreview.com/country-rankings/ef-english-proficiency-index-by-country)).

**Chinese (zh-CN, Simplified/Hans)**

Chinese managers in Egypt can use WhatsApp, but **HQ engineers and decision-makers in China cannot**.
  - On zh pages, show a **WeChat ID plus a QR code (static image)**, plus email and a +20 phone number.
  - Use a **free personal/company-staff WeChat account** ([Dragon Trail](https://dragontrail.com/resources/blog/how-to-get-a-wechat-account-from-overseas)). **Do not** register a WeChat Service (Official) Account: overseas verification costs **$99/year** ([WorldFirst](https://www.worldfirst.com/sasia/blog/marketplaces-and-platforms/how-to-sell-on-wechat-south-asia/)), which breaks the $0 rule.

  - Maps: use a static map image plus the Chinese address and GPS coordinates.
  - Video: self-host short MP4s or use poster images instead of YouTube.
  - Fonts and captcha: no Google Fonts or reCAPTCHA (self-host fonts; tech-stack.md covers form protection).
- **Content angles Chinese investors care about:**
  - "We commission and maintain **Chinese-built lines** in Egypt." Be familiar with Chinese PLC/HMI ecosystems (Wecon 维控, plus others the client actually supports) and translate Chinese machine documentation into Arabic for local operators.
  - Mention **TEDA / Sokhna / Qantara West** by name, in Chinese (泰达, 苏赫纳).

- **Typography:** Simplified Chinese. Use the system CJK font stack rather than webfonts (detailed in tech-stack.md).
- **Search engines:** Baidu dominates mainland search and ignores hreflang. Chinese staff *in Egypt* use Google, so the zh pages mainly serve visitors referred from WeChat, email and LinkedIn rather than Baidu SEO.

## 4. What local Egyptian buyers look for

### 4.1 Evidence

- **Mobile-first, though not mobile-only:** Statcounter shows **mobile 63.7 %, desktop 34.1 %, tablet 2.2 %** of Egyptian web traffic (Sept 2026) ([Statcounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/platform-market-share/desktop-mobile-tablet/egypt)). The desktop share is meaningful for engineers at work, so both layouts must be first-class.
- **Online and well-connected:** 98.2 m internet users (82.7 %), 121 m mobile connections, median mobile download 56 Mbps (+120 % y/y) ([DataReportal, Digital 2026 Egypt](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-egypt)).
- **Platforms:** Facebook 51.6 m users, YouTube 49.3 m, **LinkedIn 15 m members** ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-egypt)). **Google holds 96.4 % of search** ([Statcounter, Sept 2026](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share/all/egypt)), so Google SEO and Google Business Profile are what count locally.
- **WhatsApp is the default business chat.** No free authoritative 2025–26 penetration figure exists: DataReportal omits WhatsApp and Statista is paywalled ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/forecasts/1145037/whatsapp-users-in-egypt/)). Vendor blogs claim 52–57 m users and ">90 % of businesses use WhatsApp", **without sources** ([ChatDaddy — weak source](https://chatdaddy.tech/blog/whatsapp-marketing-egypt)). An Egyptian academic survey reports 98 % usage and 90 % daily use in its sample ([EKB journal](https://journals.ekb.eg/article_154770.html)). Treat WhatsApp as essential, and validate with the client's own lead log (share of leads arriving via WhatsApp vs phone vs Facebook).
- **Price sensitivity:** inflation of 12–15 % and a weakening pound in 2026 ([Arab Finance](https://arabfinance.com/en/news/newdetails/reviewing-egypt-economic-scene-in-h1-2026)) make cost-effective brands (LS, Wecon) and repair/retrofit (instead of replacement) attractive.
- **Engineers decide and want specs:** ~60 % of engineers are the decision-maker or dominant voice; 87 % want datasheets linked from product/service pages ([EETech 2026](https://www.eetech.com/blog/your-website-is-already-in-a-sales-meeting-you-just-arent-there)). Vendor websites are the top first-touch research source for industrial buyers ([Manufacturing Lead Generation, 2026 — weak source](https://manufacturingleadgeneration.com/industrial-buyer-behavior-statistics/)).
- **Speed wins leads:** most companies respond too slowly to online enquiries ([HBR, 2011](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads)).
